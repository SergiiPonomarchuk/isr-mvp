# PD-7 Terminology

Paper id: D-008. Status: accepted (author). Recorded: 2026-10-12, from `specs/tasks/S0-decisions-to-import.md`.
Type: intended design (terminology).

## Decision
- **Ad-hoc public** means the group formed for one issue. It replaces "microcommunity" and the issue sense of "community".
- **Organisation** or **deployment** means an NGO, co-op or DAO that uses ISR.
- **Users** means all registered users.

Firestore collection names that still say `microcommunities` are an as-built fact, not a decision.

## Note (please confirm)
`ISR_PROJECT_MANIFESTO.md` §7.6 (l.409) forbids "Organization" as a name for the ISR unit. PD-7 uses "Organisation" in a different sense: the body that deploys ISR. The two are compatible, but 14 conventions will state both senses explicitly.
